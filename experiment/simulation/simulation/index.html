Virtual Lab: Activation Functions & Optimization

Interactive demonstrations for understanding deep learning fundamentals

Learning Path

These demonstrations are designed to build intuition progressively. Start with Demo 1 and work your way through to understand how activation functions and optimizers affect neural network training.

1

Activation Function Shape Explorer

Explore the mathematical properties of different activation functions. Visualize f(x) and f'(x) to understand how derivatives enable learning and where saturation occurs.

Key Concept: Neural networks are piecewise mathematical functions, and learning depends on derivatives.
2

Gradient Flow Visualizer

See how gradients flow backward through a multi-layer network. Understand vanishing and exploding gradients, and why activation choice matters for deep networks.

Key Concept: Information can die during backpropagation if gradients vanish.
3

Optimizer Step Visualizer

Watch how different optimizers navigate the loss landscape. Compare SGD, Momentum, and Adam to see how they affect convergence speed and stability.

Key Concept: Gradients give direction; optimizers decide how to move through parameter space.
4

Learning Rate Scheduler

Discover why learning rate should change during training. Compare different scheduling strategies and their effect on convergence.

Key Concept: Learning needs to be fast early and careful later.
5

Full Training Overlay

Train real neural networks on Fashion-MNIST with different activation functions and optimizers. Compare training curves to see everything come together.

Key Concept: Modern defaults converge faster, and bad activations can't be completely saved by good optimizers.

About This Lab

This virtual lab is designed for undergraduate computer science students learning deep learning. Each demonstration builds on the previous one to create a comprehensive understanding of how activation functions and optimization algorithms work together to train neural networks.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the mathematical properties of activation functions
  • Visualize how gradients flow through neural networks
  • Compare different optimization algorithms and their behaviors
  • Learn why learning rate scheduling improves training
  • Synthesize concepts by training real models
