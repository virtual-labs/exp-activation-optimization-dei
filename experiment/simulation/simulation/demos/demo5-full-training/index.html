This demo trains real neural networks on the Fashion-MNIST dataset, allowing you to see how activation functions and optimizers interact in practice.

Fashion-MNIST Dataset

A dataset of 70,000 grayscale images of clothing items in 10 categories (T-shirts, trousers, dresses, etc.). Each image is 28×28 pixels.

Network Architecture

Simple Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP):

  • Input: 784 pixels (28×28 flattened)
  • Hidden Layer 1: 128 neurons + activation
  • Hidden Layer 2: 64 neurons + activation
  • Output: 10 classes (softmax)

What to Observe

Activation Functions:
  • Sigmoid/Tanh: Slower convergence, lower final accuracy due to vanishing gradients
  • ReLU: Fast convergence, good final accuracy
  • ELU: Similar to ReLU, sometimes smoother training
Optimizers:
  • SGD: Slower, may need higher learning rate
  • Adam: Fast, adaptive, generally best default
  • RMSprop: Good middle ground

Key Takeaways

Modern defaults (ReLU + Adam) exist for good reasons. They converge faster and reach better accuracy. However, understanding why other combinations fail helps you debug training issues in real projects.
